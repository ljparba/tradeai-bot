## 5. Executive Summary

The TradeAI v13 system holds its post-fix posture: 8.89/10 overall, essentially flat against the 2026-05-23 baseline of 8.94/10, with six dimensions improving and five regressing under deeper inspection. **No CROSS_REF fixes have been reverted.** Paper-trading operation remains SAFE — the bot is running cycle 76 in PAPER mode with heartbeat fresh and all kill switches active. The five regressions concentrate in two domains: (a) the honest-metrics + backtest-validity stack, where the active `data/signals.db` was reset since last audit and the cross-config sr_trial_std pool is now null, causing the verdict logic to silently grant DSR PASS without any multiple-testing correction; and (b) the operational-resilience layer, where the `tradeai-watchdog.service` systemd unit promised by CLAUDE.md §2 does not actually exist on disk or in `deploy/`. Four new HIGH findings emerged from this audit: a real SMT-sign contradiction between the confidence formula and template tier scoring; a `closed_at` aliasing defect in the cross-config-std compute script that silently disables CPCV purging; an embargo-anchor formula deviation under-embargoing by one label horizon; and a statistical proof that Tier A is no longer distinguishable from Tier B on n=4,132 historical signals.

**LIVE GO/NO-GO: NO-GO.** Three blockers: (1) 0/30 paper signals closed since DB reset (LIVE clearance gate); (2) `tradeai-watchdog.service` not deployed — bot is unsupervised against hangs; (3) honest-metrics verdict logic can return PASS when DSR is null, bypassing the documented `DSR ≥ 95%` LIVE gate. Paper-trading GO is unconditional — the underlying code is sound and Run-168 baseline metrics (CPCV mean WR 79.11%, DSR-when-computable 100%) remain credible against the disconnected historical pool.

**Single highest-impact improvement:** Restore the historical cross-config std pool. Merging `data/signals_baseline_run113.db` history (or seeding `bot_state.cumulative_min_trials=93`) and running `scripts/compute_cross_config_sr_std.py` would simultaneously (a) un-null the DSR card on the dashboard, (b) close the verdict-bypass logic gap by making the null-path unreachable in normal operation, (c) restore credible DSR readings to all post-reset backtests, and (d) push the Honest Metrics dimension from 8.2 back toward 9.0+. This is a data-layer fix requiring no changes to `validation.py` or `backtest.py` math.
