## NEW FINDINGS

### L-1 (LOW): FVG mitigation scan reads post-entry bars within the 5M sub-window

**Classification:** NEW FINDING
**Severity:** LOW (bias direction is CONSERVATIVE, not optimistic)
**File:** `/home/tradeai/TradeAI/ict_engine.py:333-337` (called from `crt_engine.py:278`)

When `score_ict_fvg` is called at `d = mss_bar_5m - 1` or `d = mss_bar_5m`, the mitigation check runs:
```python
if len(closes) > d + 2:
    if direction == "BUY" and any(closes[k] <= bottom for k in range(d + 2, len(closes))):
        return None
```

Here `closes` is the `c5m_win["closes"]` array, which extends `H4_CRT_MSS_HORIZON (30) + CRT_5M_HEADROOM_BUFFER (5) = 35` bars past the H4 C2 close time — including up to 35 bars AFTER the MSS event. The FVG at the MSS bar will be classified as "already mitigated" if price fills it within the next 35 × 5 = 175 minutes post-entry.

**Consequence:** A trader placing the entry at signal time would see a still-valid FVG. The backtest rejects it. This REDUCES the FVG-confluence signal count (anti-optimistic bias). WR is not inflated — signals that survive are genuinely the ones where the FVG wasn't immediately reclaimed. The practical effect is slight undercount of FVG-type CRT signals (OB-type signals are unaffected).

**Severity reasoning:** Not optimistic. Slightly deflates signal frequency, not WR. Mechanism was inherited from the 5M_SWEEP FVG scorer, which uses the same pattern. Cross-check: in live trading, the mitigation check cannot use future bars (they don't exist), so the live scanner would emit MORE FVG signals than backtest. This is a minor live/BT parity divergence on the FVG sub-path — backtest is conservative.

**Fix:** Pass a truncated `closes[:d+2]` to `score_ict_fvg` or add an explicit `max_close_idx` param. Low urgency given conservative direction.
