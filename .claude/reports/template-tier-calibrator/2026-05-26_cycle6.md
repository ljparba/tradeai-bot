## 1. NF-2 re-investigation — dr_location population

Cycle-5 NF-2 flagged Run-79 had 100% `dr_location=UNKNOWN` (all 7 surviving signals). Hypothesis: B.1 cliff artifact vs DR classifier bug.

Run-81 (n=35) distribution:
| dr_location | n | share |
|---|---|---|
| DISCOUNT | 16 | 45.7% |
| PREMIUM | 13 | 37.1% |
| UNKNOWN | 6 | 17.1% |
| EQUILIBRIUM | 0 | 0.0% |

**Verdict: B.1 CLIFF ARTIFACT — classifier is healthy.** When the DR gate is OFF the population is dominated by classified PREMIUM/DISCOUNT signals (82.9% classified); UNKNOWN sits at a plausible 17%. The 100% UNKNOWN cliff in Run-79 was selection bias — the symmetric DR-gate killed 98.5% of post-FVG/MSS candidates (per cycle-5 D2 diagnostic) and the surviving 7 were exactly those the classifier could not place. NF-2 is **CLOSED — non-defect**.

Note: EQUILIBRIUM=0 across 35 signals is consistent with the strategy's geometry (FVG retrace post-displacement rarely lands at the dealing-range midpoint).

## 3. H-C feasibility — Tier A redefinition (REACTION_CONFIRMED ∩ iFVG_present)

Run-81 features:
- `REACTION_CONFIRMED`: **3 of 35 (8.6%)** — 100% WR (3/3)
- `ifvg_present=1`: **34 of 35 (97.1%)** — non-discriminating
- Intersection (RC ∩ iFVG): **3 signals**

**Verdict: H-C NOT DATA-VALIDATABLE at n=35.** REACTION_CONFIRMED is too rare (8.6%) to drive a tier definition; the intersection with iFVG adds no information because iFVG is essentially universal (97.1%). H-C should be deferred until n≥100 or until REACTION_CONFIRMED base-rate rises. **Recommended interim H-C v2:** define Tier A as `MSS=HIGH ∩ FVG=HIGH ∩ session∈KZ ∩ dr_location∈{PREMIUM,DISCOUNT}` (drop entry_type, drop iFVG); on Run-81 that yields n≈12 with measurable separation against Tier B (per §2).
