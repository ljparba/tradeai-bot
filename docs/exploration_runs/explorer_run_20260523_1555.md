## Most Surprising Results

1. **5 of 9 executed experiments produced EXACT-IDENTICAL output to baseline** (Q-3, P-2, P-3, P-4, P-5, P-6). The system has multiple binding gates upstream of these parameters (FVG=HIGH quality, ICT_MAX_SETUP_AGE_BARS=24, MIN_TP1_MULT=1.5, confidence clamp at 10, dealing_range_gate=False). This means the operator's working "knob set" is much smaller than the public-facing parameter list suggests. Many parameters are effectively decorative under current config.

2. **Q-4 (RR gate 1.5→2.0) is catastrophic, not just suboptimal.** -36n, WR=50%, q05=0%, DSR=67.7%. The strategy depends on rr1∈[1.5,2.0] setups for ~78% of its volume. This makes raising MIN_TP1_MULT a structural impossibility without a co-change to SL placement (ATR_SL_MULT). Q-4 should be a **confirmed structural anti-pattern** in Learned Patterns, not just rejected.

3. **Q-2 (cluster_size≥2) loses MORE signals than expected** but doesn't gain WR. The Fix #9 EQH/EQL bonus was added on the premise that clustered sweeps are higher-quality targets — but at this sample size, isolated (cluster_size=1) sweeps converted at roughly the SAME rate. The bonus may be correctly weighted as a *bonus*, not a *gate* — turning it into a gate destroys 15 valid signals while gaining 0pp WR.

4. **P-1 (SWING_N 2→3) adds 3 signals at the cost of -6.7pp.** Same direction as Cycle 1 P-1b (which was reverted historically). The +3 signals are lower-quality (longer-confirmed swings have already "matured" by the time entry triggers, so price is further from the swing reference). Confirms ICT_SWING_N=2 is at the Pareto-optimal corner for current FVG=HIGH config.

## Cross-Domain Observations

**Observation 1: Macro filter not wired into backtest.py.**
**Relevant Agent:** `backtest-optimizer` or a dedicated bug-fix pass
**Reason:** `config.py:228` `MACRO_FILTER_ENABLED` is read by `crypto_alert.py:2035` but is NOT imported or consumed by `backtest.py`. This means: (a) backtest cannot evaluate Q-1 hypothesis at all, and (b) live and backtest paths diverge — any LIVE macro-filtered signal will not match its backtest counterpart. Either the macro filter should be promoted into the backtest signal loop, OR config.py should document that MACRO_FILTER is live-only. Currently the silent divergence is a backtest validity risk.

**Observation 2: Q-3 / P-2 / P-3 / P-4 / P-5 / P-6 all produce bit-identical signal counts and CPCV metrics.**
**Relevant Agent:** `backtest-optimizer` (for hypothesis-queue pruning)
**Reason:** Six of the optimizer's pre-built Tier-1 hypotheses cannot move the baseline at all under the current config. The optimizer should mark them as "no-op under FVG=HIGH+bias=none+drgate=False" and skip them in future cycles until the binding parent gate is itself changed.

**Observation 3: Q-4 RR=2.0 catastrophic failure mode is structurally tied to MIN_TP1_MULT=1.5.**
**Relevant Agent:** `backtest-optimizer`
**Reason:** ICT_MIN_RR_GATE and MIN_TP1_MULT must be moved together (or in a controlled grid). A single-param sweep of ICT_MIN_RR_GATE is fundamentally a no-op-or-catastrophe binary because of the floor relationship between MIN_TP1_MULT and rr1. Add to Learned Patterns: "ICT_MIN_RR_GATE > MIN_TP1_MULT requires paired MIN_TP1_MULT increase."
